BOX1R

RING TO BELT

Five fighting styles, a living world ranking and three title belts. Slip the jab, land the counter, take the belt. Free to play, plays offline.

WATCH THE TRAILER

Lace up the gloves and step through the ropes.

BOX1R is one-on-one boxing drawn in smooth, high-frame-count pixel art. A wide isometric camera shows the whole ring, and the fighters are small but built with real proportions, turning through 16 directions.

FOOTWORK WINS FIGHTS

The whole ring is yours. Step in, step out, circle, pivot around your opponent and flick the stick to dash. Nobody turns on a dime: circle to the side of a fighter who's shelled up and your shots go right past the guard.

HEAD MOVEMENT, NOT JUST A HIGH GUARD

Block, or raise your hands just before impact to parry. Slip, sway back, duck under, and bob and weave out of the corner. Make a punch miss and the counter is yours. Feint to freeze your opponent, or tie them up in a clinch to catch your breath.

EVERY PUNCH IN THE BOOK

Jab, cross, hook and uppercut, to the head or to the body. Add a direction for a stepping jab, an overhand or a check hook. Hooks chain left and right. Body shots drain stamina for the rest of the fight.

FIVE STYLES, FIVE FINISHERS

Out-boxer, swarmer, slugger, counter-puncher or boxer-puncher. Each style moves, punches and steps differently, with its own signature move and its own special, charged by the roar of the crowd.

HITS YOU CAN FEEL

Hit-stop, screen shake, sweat and spit, layered glove sounds and haptics. Knockdowns depend on the punch: a hook spins a fighter to the canvas, a big shot sends one flying, the ropes catch a fall in the corner. A clean counter can switch the lights off in one punch, with a slow-motion replay.

A REAL FIGHT

Two kinds of stamina, swelling and cuts that can bring a doctor stoppage, ten counts, three-knockdown TKOs, and three judges who don't always agree. Between rounds, choose your corner's work. Live commentary and punch stats show why you won or lost.

CAREER: CLIMB A LIVING RANKING

Challenge the fighters just above you. Win and take their spot; lose and you drop. The rest of the world fights too: rivals train, age and retire, new prospects debut, and belts change hands. Win the city, national and world titles, defend them, and retire into the Hall of Fame if your record earns it. Fights and camps earn experience; every level up gives training points you spend yourself. No roguelike resets.

TRAINING CAMP

Eight drills between fights: mitts with a coach calling combinations, the heavy bag, sparring against a partner who fights like your next opponent, treadmill roadwork, defensive mitts, the double-end bag, the slip rope and the jump rope.

BUILD YOUR BOXER

Set height, weight and muscle. Pick from 17 hairstyles and beards, any color for trunks, gloves and boots, patterns and tattoos (including full Japanese and Samoan pieces) earned in the ring, orthodox or southpaw, and boot styles that change how you move.

FREE TO PLAY

Ads are optional rewarded videos in the training camp, never forced. Remove ads once to get every reward without watching. The game plays offline.
